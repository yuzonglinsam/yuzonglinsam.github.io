Tickmark

A credit research workspace covering about 500 listed companies, built on SEC filings.

Each company page puts financial measures, stress tests and an AI-written credit memo side by side. Every figure links to the filing it came from. A memo sentence that disagrees with the numbers is checked, withheld, and shown next to the comparison that caught it.

Missing data stays marked as missing instead of turning into zero, and the memo text itself carries no numbers: those come from the tables.
